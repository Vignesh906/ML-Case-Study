Project Architecture & Methodology

Academic technical specification, dataset inventory, and data leakage safeguards

1. System Architecture Overview

The system utilizes a decoupled machine learning architecture where data ingestion, leakage-free feature engineering, and model training are executed offline. The trained model artifacts and scikit-learn preprocessing pipelines are persisted via joblib and served asynchronously by a high-performance Python/Flask web dashboard.

2. Dataset Provenance & Characteristics

  • Public Transit Dataset: Spans the calendar year 2020 across 5 major global cities (Berlin, London, Paris, Tokyo, Singapore). Covers Bus, Train/Rail, and Metro modalities with 88 operational features.
  • Commercial Aviation Dataset: Derived from official United States Department of Transportation (US DOT) Bureau of Transportation Statistics (BTS) 2015 airline on-time performance records (5.8M flights across 14 airlines and 322 commercial airports).
  • Target Severity Discretization:
    • ON TIME: ≤ 5.0 minutes delay
    • MODERATELY DELAYED: > 5.0 to ≤ 20.0 minutes delay
    • SEVERELY DELAYED: > 20.0 minutes delay

3. Important Limitations & Academic Integrity

Synthetic Transit Benchmark & Historical Aviation Data

The urban public transit data (Bus, Train/Rail, Metro) is a calibrated synthetic urban simulation benchmark (transit_v2) designed to evaluate multimodal machine learning pipelines under zero-inflated, right-skewed noise conditions. It does not represent live vehicle-tracking feeds from transit authorities. The commercial aviation dataset represents authentic calendar year 2015 flight records from the US Department of Transportation (DOT Form 41).

  • Live Weather Telemetry: Weather conditions and temperatures are fetched live from Open-Meteo for the current observation moment only; they are not matched to historical flight timestamps.
  • Absence of Live GPS Telemetry: The system evaluates scheduled timetable parameters and ambient meteorology; live automated vehicle location (AVL), Automatic Dependent Surveillance–Broadcast (ADS-B), and FAA ground-stop feeds are not integrated.
  • Uniform Threshold Notice: Exact delay severity thresholds (On Time: ≤ 5 min, Moderate: > 5 to ≤ 20 min, Severe: > 20 min) are strictly unified across classifiers, regressors, and the user interface.

4. Strict Data Leakage Prevention Protocol

Vulnerability Category Potential Leakage Vector Enforced Safeguard / Architecture
Post-Event Metrics Actual arrival delay, taxi-in, wheels-on Excluded entirely from training and feature matrices.
Post-Trip Status Flags service_status, on_time_flag Discarded during data ingestion. Only pre-dispatch features used.
Future Historical Delays Rolling statistics incorporating future trips Rolling means calculated strictly from prior time windows.
Temporal Splitting Random shuffle leaking future patterns Strict chronological partitioning: Train (Jan-Sep), Val (Oct), Test (Nov-Dec).
